Story 1
1. Tom was playing with his toy car in the living room.

Full story:
One day, Tom was playing with his toy car in the living room. His father came home and gave him a new toy. Tom and his sister opened the box together and played happily. Finally, his sister found the toy car near the sofa.
Story 2
1. A boy climbed a tree because his kite was stuck in the branches.
A boy climbed a tree – Một cậu bé trèo lên cây because his kite was stuck – vì chiếc diều của cậu ấy bị mắc in the branches – trên cành cây
2. Some children came to help him and gave him some ideas.
Some children came to help him – Một vài đứa trẻ đến giúp cậu ấy and gave him some ideas – và đưa cho cậu ấy vài ý tưởng
3. The boy carefully pulled the kite from the tree.
The boy carefully pulled the kite – Cậu bé cẩn thận kéo chiếc diều from the tree – ra khỏi cây
4. Finally, they flew the kite happily in the park.
Finally – Cuối cùng they flew the kite happily – họ thả diều rất vui vẻ in the park – trong công viên
Full story:
It was a nice day in the park. A boy climbed a tree because his kite was stuck in the branches. Some children came to help him and gave him some ideas. The boy carefully pulled the kite from the tree. Finally, they flew the kite happily in the park.
